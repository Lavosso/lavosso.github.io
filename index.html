Flagship project · operational

ARKHE

A Personal AI Operating Layer

My JARVIS-inspired personal AI assistant. Modular and local-first, it brings my tasks, notes, files, and developer tools into one place.

explore the architecture ↓ view components ↓ see the showcase ↓

Overview

I built Arkhe to bring the tools I use every day into one assistant.

I can work with Arkhe through the browser or by voice. The Core interprets requests and calls the modules it needs, from tracking deadlines and preparing lecture notes to working with local files and generating artifacts. Arkhe Storage keeps conversations and application data available across sessions, while Cloud Sync handles synchronization in the background.
