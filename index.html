A little context

I like understanding things by getting my hands on them.

At Gdańsk University of Technology, I am studying Automation and Robotics (semester 3) alongside a second degree focused on Industry 5.0 (semester 1).

My interests sit somewhere between software, AI, automation and physical systems. I am deliberately keeping the map wide for now, not because I want to collect technologies, but because I like seeing what happens when different fields start clicking together.

I learn something, build something, break something, write it down, and then usually find another thing I want to build.

10× beats 10%

Sometimes the best optimization is changing what the system is trying to do rather than polishing the current solution.

Best part is no part

Complexity has a cost. Removing a component can be a better engineering decision than making the component better.

mindset

Try difficult things

A recurring thought from my notes: if everything you try works, your experiments might not be ambitious enough.
